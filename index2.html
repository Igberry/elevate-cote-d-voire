Our Challenges

Côte d'Ivoire faces high unemployment (36%) and persistent poverty (39.5%), impacting living conditions and access to education. Although poverty has improved since 2015, the infant mortality rate remains high (52.4 per 1,000 live births). Health challenges include below average nutrition rates and diseases such as malaria.
